## Differences, reproduction and correction

The native implementation is `inkwave-public/src/game/character.js`: `_trackRoot` handles physical-root and stationary-treadmill velocities; `_updateSquid` uses `hs`, `sqYaw`, `t * frequency`, local quaternion damping and the squid pivot. `inkwave-public/src/game/character-mats.js`, `makeSquidMaterial`, deforms the actual indexed geometry using `sin(uTime * uWig.y + ...)` and a second oscillator at 4/5 of that frequency. `Actor._horizontal` and `_finishFrame` remain the authoritative gameplay/motion inputs.
