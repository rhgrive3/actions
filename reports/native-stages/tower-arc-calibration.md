# Local source Tower start calibration

The former local route builder used the source node nearest the arena origin as Tower's start. A detour around unchanged source geometry could leave unequal travel arcs on either side. Barnacle's cleared 36-waypoint route produced 29.4172/11.1715 units of travel and uncontested knockout times of 29.5167/14.3167 seconds with the same five-second checkpoint.

`nativeTowerStartIndex()` now selects the **existing cleared waypoint nearest half the complete 3D route arc**. Barnacle starts at index 17 rather than 9: the arcs are 20.5888/19.99997, with knockout times 22.1667/21.6667 seconds at 30/60 Hz and 22.1583/21.6667 at 120 Hz. Residual differences follow the existing waypoint spacing. No ratio threshold is used, no waypoint is moved, and no source-supported endpoint, pad, lift, deck size or non-Tower objective changes.

The original five stages, Undertow, Museum and Barnacle were measured from their exact private source catalogs. Before/after hashes match for every complete route and every other mode's objectives. Seven supported routes retain every full deck/pole/rider swept envelope, nine human capsule positions per waypoint and a normal source-supported boarding approach. Hagglefish remains explicitly unavailable for Tower. This calibration cannot qualify a same-bank route or restore Mincemeat's separately qualified unavailable mode.

Real Chromium with SwiftShader passed 21 physical W/Space boarding cases at 30/60/120 Hz at the recalibrated starts, with eight actual actors and seven bots per stage. Production Actor resolution retained 2,182 deck-edge supports at original waypoints and quarter-segment interiors. The legacy Tidewater graph retained its exact digest (4,158 candidates, 4,154 valid nodes). The rules measurements use production `RankedEngine`/`TowerRule` with controlled single-rider frames; they are separate from physical boarding evidence.

The source-wall integration fixture also distinguished a valid paint-line hold from an upward lane. Barnacle's first attached sample has zero vertical speed because the source wall above is unowned. The test verifies that cap, continues to a genuinely owned upward lane, and still requires enemy ink to release the squid. An explicit stop/resume/release unit control passes. Source geometry and runtime climb behavior are unchanged.

The complete numeric proof is [tower-arc-calibration-verification.json](tower-arc-calibration-verification.json). The private calibration build passed the existing startup, artifact, dependency, preload and service-worker budgets without changing limits. Screenshots and source/derived assets remain ignored local evidence.

Comparison status: this is a fairer **local INKWAVE calibration on inferred display-source collision**, not recovered Splatoon 3 ranked placement or a claim of 11.3.0 behavior equivalence. Nintendo's native collision, paint/climb masks, retail tower trajectories/checkpoints, exact units, moving-object states and hardware performance remain unverified. Tests use ordinary human movement, the default shooter, no gear bonuses, normal difficulty and the documented source floors; SwiftShader measurements do not establish mobile or Switch FPS.

Reproduction uses `scripts/measure-native-tower-calibration.mjs` before and after the change, `scripts/check-inkwave-native-tower-calibration.mjs` on a private prepared build, and the native integration/ranked/boarding/clearance/arc/capped-wall regressions. Asset-enabled CPU work uses the shared heavy-job lock and `--test-concurrency=1`; every Chromium call uses the shared browser lock. The eight-stage verification catalog combined the independently reviewed definitions and exact private assets without committing source media.
