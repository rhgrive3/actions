# Native source floor waypoint following

Ordinary source stages used the blockout follower's six-waypoint lookahead and stuck-recovery waypoint skip. On the unchanged Robo ROM-en source, a bot left the upper floor before the path corner; the later height guard correctly discarded its route. The existing conservative source follower now applies to actual native levels. It follows the current waypoint, verifies the original source centre and the real controller foot support before advancing, and plants through ordinary humanoid input at corners. Normal 2.4-second recovery still replans. Legacy blockout budgets, waypoint policy and malformed source opt-in rejection remain unchanged.

The physical foot predicate, Actor acceleration/braking/collision, graph, geometry, NoPaint roles, masks, opposing pads and traversal flags are unchanged. This change does not alter jump admission/input ownership, special traversal, Clams goal selection or Tower behavior. The implementation is in `patches/native-stages/adapter.mjs` and `runtime/native-bot-support.mjs`.
