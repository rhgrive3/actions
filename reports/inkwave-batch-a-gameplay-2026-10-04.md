## Validation

Focused regressions use the real adapted Actor/WeaponRunner and movement dispatcher. Combined scenarios use the existing 60Hz FixedClock under 30/60/120Hz render schedules. Tests check both held charge preservation and released-charge cancellation, shared floor/wall chain boundaries, fresh charge, lifecycle clearing, and unrelated movement paths. Parent review rejects agent reports where the actual diff or source contradicts them.

Final focused counts, baseline failures and exact-SHA full validation/browser receipts are recorded in the PR. CPU logic tests, CI Chromium/WebKit browser evidence and physical Switch/iOS comparisons are separate proof classes. Physical hardware comparisons remain unperformed.

The guide also describes firing while returning to kid form. This batch preserves INKWAVE's existing emergeDelay input gate: a release before the store is restored cancels it; holding ZR through restoration and then releasing fires the full shot. The exact ZL/ZR ordering within one simulation tick and a return-fire gesture during emergeDelay are not certified here. Those resurfacing/readiness semantics require independent reference measurement and coordination with PR #63, rather than silently changing its pending timing work.
