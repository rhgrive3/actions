## Installed discrepancy and narrow correction

Before this correction, `DIRECTORS.trizooka.frame` polled `s3Trizooka.shots` and emitted `trizookaMuzzle` once when the action counter increased. Remote `ROUND_FX.trizooka.start` emitted it only for lobe0. That recipe made all three shock discs in one loop and one `.14`-second white core. Thus the already-installed actual native birth sequence at 0/4/8 source frames was not reflected in the muzzle effect. The first observed action could also be missed if its counter was already incremented when its actor track initialized.

Only `patches/splatoon3/runtime/special-vfx.mjs` and a Trizooka-only read-only accessor in `special-motion.mjs` change at runtime:

- A Trizooka-named observer reads accepted native `_push`, accepted ghost append and actual pending→born `_step` transitions. Existing native methods, arguments and returns remain the owners of admission, clocks, collisions and networking. A new per-director WeakSet deduplicates nested observations and resets only when native `_new` acquires that projectile for a new use.
- The Trizooka round's existing `when` gate excludes unborn/cancelled globs, including their premature trails. Round-start is a zero-age fallback for native tick-endpoint flush births that do not call `_step`.
- Each accepted glob emits one of the existing three ring records and one brief white core. The core's 3/60-second lifetime and zero fade-in are explicit INKWAVE presentation calibration, not extracted Nintendo constants. Heavy muzzle droplets/smoke/streaks/petals remain once on lobe0, not tripled.
- Every cosmetic pulse resolves the current held nozzle through `trizookaPresentationMuzzle(character,out)`. It accepts only the actor-owned current Trizooka mapping, exact registered/current model parented to that character’s kid, visible model and all visible ancestors, and finite local/transformed coordinates. It refreshes the world matrix read-only. Missing, hidden, retired, detached, wrong-owner or invalid models fall back to a copy of finite native `p.start`. It does not call or change native `getMuzzle`/`getAimMuzzle`, launch snapshots, poses, transforms or gameplay.
- Actor-counter inference and the separate remote-lobe0 muzzle trigger are removed. Dead, pending, cancelled, rejected, ended or wrong-system observations do not emit. Births observed while effects are disabled are remembered without drawing, so re-enabling does not replay them. Clearing presentation also does not replay still-live rounds.

No kit/collision/physics/damage/movement/paint/network/event-count or timestamp code changes. No character, hair, held-device geometry, model-detail or shared shader edits. No browser launch, Work operation, download or publication.

## Follow-on expected-owner alias guard

The first current-nozzle freeze `d071d0b40347eb69989093ea058c14616641a6fa` remains immutable but is superseded for final acceptance. Independent combined-candidate QA found that actor A's stale `character` reference could point at a live character B whose own actor/model ownership was internally consistent. The accessor correctly validated B, but the caller had not verified that B's expected owner was A. Its cosmetic flash therefore borrowed B's nozzle `(40,8.075,-19.598)` instead of A's native snapshot `(2,3,4)`.

The follow-on runtime correction is one caller condition only: reject `a.character?.actor !== a` before asking the Trizooka-only accessor for its current modeled nozzle. On rejection, copy the existing finite native `p.start` fallback. No accessor, gameplay, birth schedule, timing, state or model change.

Two new explicit local/ghost regressions use a live, visible, internally valid foreign Trizooka character. They check all three fallback pulses, unchanged native snapshots and unchanged birth-record counts. Both fail against the exact d071 runtime with the foreign-coordinate mismatch. Focused source and transformed fixtures now pass16/16 each; the related existing regressions remain31/31. Upstream compatibility also passes. The prior14-case verification remains historical evidence for d071, not the final guard's case count. GPU/browser visual acceptance and full-site budget remain unverified.
