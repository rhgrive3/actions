# Independent verification of special presentation

The initial Crab acceptance in this record is superseded by [the corrected direction verification](crab-direction-verification.md). The earlier review missed backwards knees and above-hip ankles; final forward-bend evidence includes actual signed joint checks and front/side views with the chassis hidden. Reef and other earlier results retain their stated scope.

Historical source baseline `fd6435a4dd2781928d211537c485c87bc50b3784` used the fetched Three.js assets from the full checkout. With `INKWAVE_UPSTREAM_SOURCE=/workspace/actions/inkwave-public`, the focused special presentation, native controls, models, motion, VFX, paint-envelope and projectile paint-credit tests passed 70 cases. The separate VFX recipe-parity file imports the local vendor module directly; after temporarily exposing the same vendor files it passed four cases. The initial missing-vendor errors were setup errors, not candidate failures. `python3 -m unittest scripts/tests/test_compare_inkwave_special_presentation.py` passed four tests.

The controlled browser driver `scripts/render-inkwave-special-trajectory.mjs` checks every built artifact against its manifest, seeds `Math.random`, loads the actual built Character and source-motion bank, and steps Actor, Physics, Projectiles and FX on a flat floor. It records hand, foot, hip and chest positions and images from front and side at nominal quarter-second intervals, both with and without FX. At 30 Hz the nearest actual step is recorded alongside the nominal time. Crab measurements use the midpoints of the authored black bar geometry; Reef measurements derive slot centers from the rendered model's `hole` geometry and compare them with actual hand-hole points transformed through the source-loaded skeleton. These are controlled local-engine measurements, not real-world FPS, a retail frame comparison, or proof of Switch parity.

The browser standard special checker additionally covers pooled VFX saturation, eviction, clear, reuse and model lifecycle. It does not directly render all six changed round terminal cases. The terminal lifecycle tests from the effects lane separately exercise native collision and cleanup boundaries, including 30/60/120 Hz, FX-on/off gameplay and emitted module execution; see `special-fx.md` for their commands and limits. The splash lane's actual inkBlast cadence and emitted-module tests are documented in `splash.md`.

Candidate built content hash `2085016a5f5bf301f5161147f1fb3850830ce1ec4adda224e3b52481a627982d`: Chromium software-WebGL driver completed at 30 and 120 simulation steps per second with 128×128 images. All three source-motion rigs were ready, and their FX directors reported zero errors. Eight sampled hand-hole distances to Crab bar midpoints were 0.0225 game units; Reef distances to independently extracted slot bounding centers ranged approximately 0.0215–0.0321 game units. These are distances to the chosen geometric centers, not collision or penetration tests. Evidence is under `/workspace/actions-verify-evidence/candidate-30-128/` and `candidate-120-128/`.

The direct-dt fixture observed Reefslider's position differ by 0.28875 game units between 30 and 120 Hz during the dash at 1.0 and 1.5 seconds; phases matched and the position converged by 2 seconds. This fixture does not run the game's normal fixed-clock scheduling, and it does not by itself attribute that difference to this change. Do not interpret the visual checks as cross-rate gameplay-physics equivalence; the separate same-rate FX/presentation-on versus off tests address the gameplay invariant.

The 480×480 matched 60 Hz review compared current main `5d4c27d` (content hash `276c8c285f5ab7533d242bee140ab4e07f43204d645e19c877c7c1102bfe5b79`) with interim candidate content hash `2085016a5f5bf301f5161147f1fb3850830ce1ec4adda224e3b52481a627982d`. Both used Chromium 154.0.8037.57, seed 20261030 and driver SHA-256 `f24cbff1a4cd3e6c0e0bf25a02fe5fb2eec4e8ad4332fc4fc446b14107beac52`. At each of eight samples from 0.25 to 2 seconds, phase and Actor position and velocity matched exactly for Crab Tank, Reefslider and Ultra Stamp. This is a sampled controlled comparison, not a complete gameplay trace. Both browser runs reported source motion ready and no director or browser errors.

At 60 Hz the Crab hand-hole to black-bar midpoint distances decreased from 0.0632–0.0739 units on main to 0.0225 units on the interim candidate. Reef hand-hole to slot-fill bounding center decreased from 0.0481–0.1399 units to 0.0217–0.0317 units. The slot fill is intentionally offset from the black handle center by roughly 0.024 units, so this measurement is not the residual to the grip target and does not itself prove whether the hand wraps the handle. The interim side images show a raised seated Crab rider with feet near the rear steps and a crouched Reef rider; the interim Reef startup grip is under separate review for a small arm reach shortfall.
