# Crab rider direction verification


The earlier assessment above missed the backward-folded Crab rider. Its acceptance of the Crab silhouette is superseded by this review. In the old pose the source-loaded thigh and knee were at forward coordinates −0.101 and −0.111; the ankle was at height 1.020 while the thigh was at 0.943. The knee and ankle were therefore folded behind and above the hip. The previous check that the knee was ahead of the foot did not detect this direction relative to the hip.

The correction was reviewed against the official Crab reference in controlled Chromium 154.0.8037.57 software WebGL, seed 20261030, with the same driver and camera settings. The previous emitted build has content hash `2d63aa0e01a04c07166825cb7859d603f47562dc5a99301fb1fd853a96bd3368`; the corrected candidate has content hash `62b4b3dc8d2ae88277991cbe318b0adec6ff554952d6b18fb3a1cd9c36886c3c`. The candidate's `special-motion.mjs` input SHA-256 is `5feb6b0bbe883a0aa6d8f6b2de1af69ff0bf9c8a639ae1fdfbcdc51181bacfa6`; both runs verified all served artifact hashes. No browser or VFX director errors occurred.

The corrected body leans toward the controls, with knees forward of the thighs and ankles below the hips. `crab-rider-forward-geometry.test.mjs` measures displayed source-loaded bones projected onto the actor's forward axis, at 0° and 90° yaw; the old emitted build fails and the corrected emitted build passes. In the candidate, shoulders are 0.129 and head 0.172 units ahead of the pelvis; each knee is 0.126 ahead of its thigh, with the ankle 0.294 below the thigh and 0.107 below the knee. The thresholds describe this rig's visual direction, not published retail joint positions. At eight sampled times in the same 60 Hz browser fixture, candidate and previous actor position, velocity and phase match exactly. This is a sampled observation; the dedicated native gameplay-invariant test covers longer traces. The feet are hidden by the chassis in the normal view, so footrest contact remains unverified. The pictures and measurements do not establish Switch parity.

The independent geometry test was run against emitted modules using `INKWAVE_BUILT_SITE=<site>/_versions/<revision> node --experimental-vm-modules --test patches/splatoon3/tests/crab-rider-forward-geometry.test.mjs`. Against the earlier build both yaw cases failed on the shoulder, head, knee and ankle direction checks; against the candidate both passed. This independently executed check is emitted-artifact evidence. The source tests and their gameplay traces are reported separately by the motion lane. Browser captures used `node scripts/render-inkwave-crab-pose.mjs --site <built-site> --out <evidence-dir> --browser-executable /usr/bin/chromium`; its driver SHA-256 was `54ff8d949d387fc9f3e6e8326f3fc5e4408e25056999014218df8d242df6e964` for both runs.
