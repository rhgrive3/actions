The existing [special VFX comparison](../../patches/splatoon3/reference/special-vfx.md) uses a third-party [all-specials video](https://youtu.be/PLxqXn_E898), observed as 320×180 storyboards at roughly 1.98-second intervals; the game's recorded version, per-weapon gear and inputs are unknown. These samples support broad silhouette and effect observations, not frame-accurate timing, exact particle counts, or control parity. The [model reference](../../patches/splatoon3/reference/special-models.md) uses promotional and rotating images for estimated geometry; it notes the Crab Tank pilot discrepancy. The [motion comparison](../../patches/splatoon3/reference/special-motion-comparison-2026-10-03.md) records that Tidal Slam is only an approximate mapping to Triple Splashdown, and that original control conditions and joint curves are unknown.

Nintendo's [Crab Tank announcement](https://www.nintendo.com/us/whatsnew/new-splatoon-3-details-from-the-squid-research-lab/) confirms separate rapid-fire and wide-blast attacks, crab walking and faster ball movement. Nintendo's [Chill Season 2023 article](https://www.nintendo.com/jp/topics/article/fa3d0720-7dae-476d-badd-24a738ef8dac) and its static [Triple Splashdown render](https://assets.topics.apps-jp.nintendo.com/image/2023/11/21051811887902/1280/05.jpg) establish two additional ink fists and an airborne lead-knee/trailing-foot silhouette; the exact game version, gear, input and timing for the render are undisclosed. Nintendo's [update history](https://en-americas-support.nintendo.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/61257/) discusses Ink Vac shots inking ground or walls depending on impact position; it does not specify the appearance, normal, or particle timing of the visual splash.

## Prioritized findings

1. **Crab pilot stance and grip.** `SPECIAL_DEVICES.crabTank` in `runtime/special-presentation.mjs` explicitly uses a standing kid with the cockpit at waist/chest height, while `special-models.md` records the original pilot sitting on top. Reproduce by activating Crab Tank, looking from the side and obliquely while stationary and walking, then switching to the ball and back. The baseline solves hands to `CRAB_TANK_GRIPS`; the standing silhouette and relation of pilot, controls and shell remain visibly different even when the hands reach. Ball mode hides the kid. Verify any correction at both grips and across transition, locomotion and visibility; the original seat height, joint angles and timing are still estimates.
2. **Stamp throw animation depends on render cadence.** `stampArc()` uses a fixed `0.25` interpolation for every `aimThrow` update, and `pose()` calls it without `dt`. At 30/60/120 updates per second the same elapsed time yields different angles, and even `dt=0` advances it. Reproduce holding throw aim with identical duration at those rates and while paused. This is a local presentation defect, not a measured discrepancy in Nintendo's frame curve. The existing VFX reference observes an overhead wind-up and impact, but leaves the full-body joint motion unconfirmed. Reefslider lean and Tenta launcher/lid interpolations use `min(1, dt * rate)`: they freeze at zero, yet their trajectories also vary with step size. Compare equal elapsed times and retain intentional calibration when improving them.
3. **Potential phantom splash on removal.** `Director.updateRounds()` in `runtime/special-vfx.mjs` calls `finishRound(r, true)` whenever a tracked non-Inkjet round is no longer observed; `finishRound` invokes its `ROUND_FX` impact recipe at the last seen position. Disappearance can include cleanup or recycling, not necessarily a collision. Inkjet already uses a specific `inkjetImpact` terminal observation and treats disappearance as non-impact. Reproduce with a tracked special round removed/cleared before collision and with a real impact, including replicated cleanup; check the visual and gameplay event count separately. This finding is inferred from control flow and needs execution to establish which cleanup paths reach it.
4. **Surface orientation is not established by the blast recipe.** `Director.inkBlast()` always places a `DISC.SPLAT` above the impact point with `this.up` as its normal; several `ROUND_FX` impacts call it with a last position alone. A wall impact may therefore show a horizontal visual puddle, even though Nintendo's update history confirms an Ink Vac shot can ink walls. Test a confirmed ground versus vertical-wall impact in game and assess whether collision normals are available at the actual terminal event. The Nintendo note supports surface interaction, not a claim about the exact original visual effect. Preserve separation of decorative effects from actual paint.

Existing footage observations also leave Reefslider explosion height/shape, Inkjet jet width/landing, and Ultra Stamp full-body motion unresolved. The prior comparison reports a crouched rider and bow spray at 102.8 seconds, not exact timing. Matching a broad silhouette or a local regression test should not be recorded as original parity without a controlled capture of original version, weapon, gear, state and input, and an equivalent production-browser capture.
