## Findings against references and actual source

- Dapple Dualies (`s3-maneuver-short`) and Glooga Dualies (`s3-maneuver-gallon`): the alleged left-only reversal is not reproduced in the current main model. The toothbrush and hose are authored on X=0, so an X mirror would not change their central position. Actual Actor/Runner firing measurements put the toothbrush at right (-.086,.853,.176) and left (.093,.855,.183), and the Glooga hose at right (-.084,.927,.242), left (.091,.928,.246). Both muzzles point forward. No speculative mirroring mechanism is added. Idle/walk/fire and both side-view images are required for the final visual conclusion; source checks alone are not retail parity.
- Ballpoint (`s3-spinner-downpour`): main has no transparent shell at any distance. It approximates one with opaque longitudinal struts/rings, and an oversized opaque ink cylinder covers the six internal barrels. This is not specifically a far-LOD material failure. Replace these with one transparent sleeve and six animated ink tubes, preserving the grip, muzzle and part pivots.
- Standard Sloshing Machine (`s3-slosher-launcher`): current front frame/bezel already uses neutral body vertex colours. Keep it unchanged. PR1246's standard `refs/s3-slosher-launcher.3d.png` is graphite/silver/cream; its `rotsheets/s3-slosher-launcher.rot.jpg` is the blue Neo variant. Do not transfer that variant's paint onto the standard model.

## Rendering contract and cost

The shared optional `glass` geometry is never merged into opaque body/ink channels. It stays the same 256-triangle mesh at near and far LOD. One singleton physical material uses base opacity .22 with a bounded grazing-angle alpha increase up to .68, FrontSide, depth test on, depth write off, ordinary transparent sorting and no shadow casting. Transmission is zero: no screen-space refraction pass. One additional nonempty beauty draw per visible Ballpoint is expected (the native AO gate also issues a zero-count API call), with fewer total model triangles after deleting the pseudo-shell bars/rings and oversized fill. Shared material and geometry survive Actor disposal and are reused after switching weapons; no per-frame geometry/material allocation is added.

## Visual refinement and measured status

The first candidate fcb859b956b8ff451943f91daea91ee1be43c9f4 used a straight 64-triangle sleeve. Its actual production shader rendered transparent pixels on black/white backgrounds ([15,17,19], [214,216,218]), and its internal tubes became visible. However, the shell outline was too weak on white, while the old broad black front cone obscured the reference's clear shoulder. Shader pixel success alone was not accepted as visual success.

The revised candidate extends the clear sleeve over the rounded front shoulder and retains only the small opaque tip. An inexpensive view-angle alpha term gives the silhouette more reflection without a transmission pass, duplicate surface, outline draw or texture. This is a limited shell/black-tip boundary correction grounded in the normal 3D reference and multi-angle Nouveau shared form, not a general rebuild. Grip, muzzle [0,.07,.432], pivots and non-shell geometry remain protected. Total authored model triangles are 3741 versus original 5276 (about 29% fewer); this is not a frame-time claim.

Initial exact-head CI run 38222002330 passed source/emitted contracts and all visual renders, but its combined 30-minute job timed out during the last A repeat. The partial ABBA results are retained as incomplete and cannot establish a performance pass. In its eight-mixed-Actor workload, two Ballpoints added two draws: game 114 to 116, far 66 to 68. The far second B run was slower than the first baseline, so triangle reduction does not establish lower runtime cost. The superseded flat-shell version will not be adopted.

The revised candidate splits visual/contracts and complete latest-main ABBA into independent CI jobs, with 30/45-minute budgets. Final browser visual acceptance, shell centre/edge pixels, and all four performance runs remain pending until that exact revision is tested. A2 completion and same-runner before/after distributions are required. Actual-device FPS, full live multiplayer and measured retail behavior remain unverified.

Self-review identified a production-specific risk beyond the studio: native GTAO replaces mesh materials with opaque normals/depth and ignores ordinary alpha. The next candidate follows the existing native/Flow/Wall draw-range gate for the transparent shell. It restores the original shared geometry count after each draw and recovers on the next beauty draw even if a render was interrupted. The shell casts no shadows. Source contracts cover two Actors sharing the same geometry, repeated normal/depth and beauty transitions, restored range start/count and Actor disposal.

The next browser gate uses real GTAOPass Normal, Depth and AO pixel comparisons with two shared-geometry shells visible versus hidden. A deliberately bypassed gate must expose the opaque-occluder counterexample. It also renders real Actors through the emitted game Renderer high-quality HDR/MSAA, GTAO, bloom, colour-grade and output stack at near/far, dark/light backgrounds and both ink colours. The full stack must add one nonempty beauty draw plus the native gate's zero-count AO API call for the shell and retain the shared range. These production AO/image assertions are pending until the revised commit runs; studio success alone is insufficient. These controlled scenes still do not represent a full live multiplayer frame-time measurement.

Eight mixed Actors, including two Ballpoints; 600px Chromium SwiftShader, 20 warm-up and 60 measured frames per tier and repeat. A/A2 versus B/B2 render-complete medians: game 1.3/1.4 ms versus 1.3/1.6 ms; far .9/.8 ms versus 1.3/1.4 ms. Far p95 is 1.3/1.2 ms versus 2.7/2.7 ms. Both B far runs are slower, so this is a visible cost, not a performance improvement. It remains a controlled renderer workload, not full-game device FPS.

Draw calls: game 114 to 116, far 66 to 68, exactly one extra draw per Ballpoint. Scene triangles: game 312618 to 309548; far 84022 to 84234. Visible geometry bytes: game 2275386 to 2237456; far 625166 to 632482. The lower authored model triangle count does not remove transparent draw/sorting cost. Final AO-gated-head ABBA and full post-stack checks must be reviewed separately before adoption.

The final ABBA also measures the same eight posed Actors and camera through the emitted high-quality production Renderer at 600px, including HDR/MSAA, GTAO, bloom and grade, with 12 warm-up and 24 measured GPU-complete frames per repeat/tier. This separately captures post-stack cost; studio and full-post numbers are never pooled. The full stack may add only one nonempty beauty draw per visible Ballpoint, with no AO fragments or shadow draw. The native gate additionally issues one zero-count AO API call, which has nonzero CPU/driver overhead and is counted explicitly.

The initial AO test revision 3d976 stopped at its deliberately unguarded depth counterexample, so its ABBA is incomplete. The test had reused the shell pixel probe's orthographic camera, while Three's GTAO Depth debug shader keeps a perspective-only conversion. For .95/1.12 view depths in that .01–10 interval, both debug values quantize to 255, masking the real depth difference. The diagnostic now uses the game's PerspectiveCamera with a .5–2 interval and records safe/unsafe changed-pixel counts on failure. No acceptance threshold, runtime material or model shape was relaxed. Every shared shell range is also checked after every full eight-Actor post-stack frame.

## Perspective fault-control result and raw draw accounting

Revision 258ea4 passed the real GTAO positive/negative control: guarded Normal/Depth/AO each change zero pixels when the two shells are shown, while beauty changes 2944 pixels. Bypassing the gate changes Normal 2944, Depth 2944 and AO 1152 pixels. Shared ranges restore correctly. This verifies the gate prevents the production opaque-occluder failure.

All four 258ea4 ABBA renders completed, but the final raw-draw budget assertion failed: actual production calls are game 247 to 251 and far 151 to 155. Inspection of the vendored Three r186 indexed renderer shows it still issues and counts gl.drawElements with count=0. The native draw-range gate suppresses geometry, not that zero-count API submission. The revised test directly observes indexed/array/instanced GL calls, separates positive-count draws from zero-count calls, and reconciles their sum with renderer.info.calls. For two Ballpoints, expected production deltas are +2 nonempty beauty draws, +2 zero-count AO calls, +4 raw calls. No runtime shader/shape or gate is altered to make this metric pass. Zero-count calls still carry CPU/driver cost.

258ea4 full-post-stack render-complete medians in A/B/B2/A2 order: game 2.1/2.3/2.1/2.4 ms, far 1.9/1.8/1.8/2.3 ms. Studio far was .9/.9/.8/.8 ms on this runner, unlike the earlier a54 run's persistent increase. Keep both observations; do not pool runs or claim faster rendering from these distributions. This four-render result is not an overall CI pass, because raw-call accounting and single-Actor full-post images still require the corrected diagnostic to complete.
