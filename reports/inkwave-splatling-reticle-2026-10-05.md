HUD previously spread all normalized charge across one ring, hiding the first-stage boundary. The build adapter adds a second concentric ring and maps the first ring to0..firstChargeTime and the second tofirstChargeTime..chargeTime from the actual local weapon profile. It does not change weapon timing, damage, ink, stream duration, projectile physics or motion.

Native WeaponRunner.charge changes meaning during streaming: it is remaining time divided by that particular release's burstDur, near1 after every partial release. Feeding it straight into both stages would incorrectly jump a partial first-circle release to full. The HUD therefore reads native remaining burstT against the existing burstFirst/burstMax piecewise curve while streaming. Partial releases stay in the first ring and full streams unwind the second before the first. No extra simulation or producer state is added. Progress storage is reused per HUD; weapon changes rebuild both nodes and reset scalar caches.
