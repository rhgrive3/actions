# Splatoon 3 special-action parity audit — 11 October 2026

## Scope and source

This report covers the 13 special-action machines implemented by `patches/splatoon3/runtime/all-specials.mjs`. It compares their runtime paths with the pinned Splatoon 3 11.3.0 parameter extraction at Leanny/splat3 commit [`7280ff9cde8bb1c5dcef46c700c326471584d2e6`](https://github.com/Leanny/splat3/commit/7280ff9cde8bb1c5dcef46c700c326471584d2e6), extracted under `source-reference/Splatoon3-resources/splat3/data/parameter/1130/weapon/`. The module header lists the 13 source tables. Public mechanics references are linked in the relevant rows below where raw parameter fields do not establish gameplay interpretation.

The pinned tables establish raw values. They do not establish the conversion from engine distance to INKWAVE world units, collision/update order, or every attack’s retail behavior. Those mappings remain labeled calibrated or unverified. This is not a full parity certification.

## Shared object-damage behavior

The 11.3.0 `misc/spl__DamageRateInfoConfig.pp__CombinationDataTableData.json` matrix is vendored in `patches/splatoon3/tests/fixtures/catalogue-object-rates-1130.json`; a separate check covers 115 attack rows by nine target columns and the `ObjectEffect_Up` multiplier. Special and native splash bursts route into deployed special/sub-device HP with phase-specific causes, skip ghost/replayed authority, and preserve `ShockSonar_Wave`, bomb-core, direct projectile, Stamp swing/throw, Killer Wail `MicroLaser`, Screen `Chimney`, Inkjet `Jetpack_Jet`, and Crab `Chariot_Body` distinctions. Focused runtime coverage checks representative Blaster, Trizooka, Inkjet and Ink Vac burst paths, Wail device hits through walls, Screen plane contact, Inkjet exhaust at 30/60/120Hz, and Crab/Stamp actor-armor phases.

Triple Splashdown’s `BulletPogo` column is 1.0 for every attack row, so its existing `ObjectEffect_Up` 1.1 factor is numerically exact for the fist target. No `catalogue.mjs` change was needed; applying the helper there would not change the table result and would add network-path risk without a confirmed discrepancy.

## Verification and status

`node --check patches/splatoon3/runtime/all-specials.mjs` passes. `node --experimental-vm-modules --test patches/splatoon3/tests/all-specials.test.mjs` passes **33/33** (31 existing cases plus the new Super Chump expiry and Zipcaster trail/cross cases). Coverage includes Wave Breaker emitted-front survival/device HP, source-phase object rates, ordinary/native object blast routing, Booyah Bomb 30/60/120Hz invariance, source-backed Tenta timing, Chump/Zip paint channels, Inkjet exhaust at 30/60/120Hz, Screen device contact, Killer Wail piercing device hits, Crab body/cannon phases, Reefslider startup/cancel/contact, and Kraken charge-release behavior.

Known gaps above remain open. The largest source-backed gaps are Tenta screen-space lock radii/circle distributions and full lock window; Inkjet splash-around velocity tiers; Reefslider splash-around/rut paint; Zipcaster source hook speed and true surface pull; Kraken `Dash_InvalidFrame` semantics; and calibrated movement, collision, paint shape, and world-unit conversions. The Super Chump and Zipcaster paint channels are now covered by focused behavior tests. This is not a full parity certification; source-field coverage remains `NOT_COMPLETE` until the remaining consumers and unproven mappings are resolved.

## Final launch and paint consumer pass

NiceBall and UltraStamp now consume source launch Z/Y clamp, gravity and per-frame position resistance. Crab shooter consumes 3.36/F launch, seven straight frames, 2.232/F transition cap and .016/F² gravity; cannon consumes the eight-frame straight and one-frame brake/free force tiers. A real Actor-launch/Projectiles-step regression compares all four flights at 30/60/120Hz. Zipcaster consumes 3.36/F for outbound hook timing before the existing calibrated player pull. Reefslider consumes 1.2 rut radius/3F stamps and splash radius/velocity tiers; Inkjet consumes splash velocity and pitch tiers. Satellite landing uses a calibrated four-frame projection because retail launch/landing geometry is not supplied. These consumers do not certify retail flight or paint geometry.

Latest-main Object Shredder identity and native Sprinkler authority are retained. Object proposals transmit raw damage, source cause and equipment identity; admitted owners apply source and gear once. The final merged suite replaces the earlier 33-case checkpoint.
