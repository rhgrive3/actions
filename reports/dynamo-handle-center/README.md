## Measurement before modification

Source: [S3 Dynamo turntable](https://splatoonwiki.org/wiki/File:S3_Dynamo_Roller_model_rotating.mp4), [standard-kit render](https://cdn.wikimg.net/en/splatoonwiki/images/5/5e/S3_Weapon_Main_Dynamo_Roller.png). Video SHA-256 and manually digitized endpoints are in `measurements.json`. The 0s front and 6s rear frames were rotated to level the drum axis and overlaid with a 50px grid. Their handle-axis intersection lies at 54.03% and 48.14% of the projected drum width. These are projected pixel ratios, not camera-calibrated 3D dimensions: endpoint reading, perspective and non-coplanar parts remain. The 3.5s near-axis view checks depth ordering, not width. Image darkness is not used as a material estimate.

In the model, the physical handle centreline is X=0, whereas the 1.821-wide drum was centred at X=-0.37. The offset is exactly 20.3185% of width, independent of camera; the source opposite views support a near-midline shaft rather than this large displacement. The requested correction sets the visual drum centre to X=0: offset 0.37 → 0. The gear/bearing ends and top guard follow the same axle coordinate, while the cross rod extends from its unchanged clamp to the repositioned gear.

## Scope and invariants

- Dynamo-only scope: centre the visual drum, then reconstruct the separate offset drive/generator, pierced support plates, exposed shaft and cone, stator teeth and copper windings. Source-visible copper and pale teal case details use existing metal/satin material classes; image darkness is not treated as black paint.
- Physical grips remain `[0,0,-0.022]` and `[0,0,0.14]`. Derived palm/IK frames remain byte-identical; they intentionally include a palm offset and are not the physical shaft centreline.
- Fold pivot `[0,0,0.88]`, drum radius 0.19, width 1.821 and spinning local drum/cap geometry remain unchanged.
- Logical muzzle `[-0.37,0,1.13]` is explicitly separated from the visual axle to avoid moving an existing gameplay/VFX socket.
- The unchanged upper clamp overlaps its outboard support at X=0.135…0.15; the transmission shaft overlaps that support by 0.035 and the rear cone by 0.03. Two pierced plates overlap the drive rims and the separate drum bearing. A small bearing-collar tab connects the top guard. No hand frame is translated to compensate for the visual defect.

The normal separation between the visible drum and drive axes is 95.35 / 108.35 pixels; local drum diameters are 105.48 / 106.10 pixels. Normalizing by the existing 0.38 drum diameter gives projected setbacks 0.344 / 0.388. The construction value is 0.36, so the drive axis is at Z=0.77 rather than the drum's Z=1.13. Y=0 is a common-plane construction assumption, not a reconstructed exact source ordinate.

Projected housing/drum diameter ratios are 1.33 / 1.21; outer case diameter 0.50 ±0.05 is the manual estimate. The axial front core depth estimate is 0.208 and rear cone length 0.348; code uses 0.21 and 0.34. These are procedural meshes, not hidden end-cap discs or borrowed retail assets.

The visible stator test casts 24 rays from the outside through its aperture and requires at least 18 first hits to be copper. ### Bearing contact refinement

A post-publication cross-section check of the thin-fork candidate found that its steel drum collar began beyond the rear bearing plate, and the taper narrowed before reaching the front plate's 0.070-radius hole. At radial Y=-0.074 and Z=1.13, actual steel triangle intersections spanned X=0.91050…1.03582, short of the plate centre planes X=0.89550 / 1.07550. The regression fails on that candidate. Extending the existing collar backward by 0.025 and retaining the taper width gives X=0.88550…1.11350; both plate planes now intersect steel outside the hole. Vertex/triangle counts are unchanged. This is a local support-contact correction, not a socket, fold or spinning-drum change.

The contact-corrected composition passes 26/26, zero skips, against the SHA-verified main ed3a046 overlay (the native-stage/build update has no direct weapon geometry or LOD changes). Body/whole-model budgets remain 13,121 / 15,978 triangles. Existing far clustering changes discontinuously to 1,123 body triangles, versus 756 on the earlier thin-fork candidate and 718 on original main; this increase is recorded rather than changing the shared LOD algorithm or target.
