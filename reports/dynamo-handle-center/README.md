## Measurement before modification

Source: [S3 Dynamo turntable](https://splatoonwiki.org/wiki/File:S3_Dynamo_Roller_model_rotating.mp4), [standard-kit render](https://cdn.wikimg.net/en/splatoonwiki/images/5/5e/S3_Weapon_Main_Dynamo_Roller.png). Video SHA-256 and manually digitized endpoints are in `measurements.json`. The 0s front and 6s rear frames were rotated to level the drum axis and overlaid with a 50px grid. Their handle-axis intersection lies at 54.03% and 48.14% of the projected drum width. These are projected pixel ratios, not camera-calibrated 3D dimensions: endpoint reading, perspective and non-coplanar parts remain. The 3.5s near-axis view checks depth ordering, not width. Image darkness is not used as a material estimate.

In the model, the physical handle centreline is X=0, whereas the 1.821-wide drum was centred at X=-0.37. The offset is exactly 20.3185% of width, independent of camera; the source opposite views support a near-midline shaft rather than this large displacement. The requested correction sets the visual drum centre to X=0: offset 0.37 → 0. The gear/bearing ends and top guard follow the same axle coordinate, while the cross rod extends from its unchanged clamp to the repositioned gear.

## Scope and invariants

- Dynamo-only scope: centre the visual drum, then reconstruct the separate offset drive/generator, pierced support plates, exposed shaft and cone, stator teeth and copper windings. Source-visible copper and pale teal case details use existing metal/satin material classes; image darkness is not treated as black paint.
- Physical grips remain `[0,0,-0.022]` and `[0,0,0.14]`. Derived palm/IK frames remain byte-identical; they intentionally include a palm offset and are not the physical shaft centreline.
- Fold pivot `[0,0,0.88]`, drum radius 0.19, width 1.821 and spinning local drum/cap geometry remain unchanged.
- Logical muzzle `[-0.37,0,1.13]` is explicitly separated from the visual axle to avoid moving an existing gameplay/VFX socket.
- The unchanged upper clamp overlaps its outboard support at X=0.135…0.15; the transmission shaft overlaps that support by 0.035 and the rear cone by 0.03. Two pierced plates overlap the drive rims and the separate drum bearing. A small bearing-collar tab connects the top guard. No hand frame is translated to compensate for the visual defect.

## Separate drive assembly correction after user review

`drive-measurements.json` records raw landmarks and their uncertainty from the same verified standard-kit turntable. Front (0s) and rear (6s) images are rotated -40.52° / 47.66° to level the drum axis. The normal separation between the visible drum and drive axes is 95.35 / 108.35 pixels; local drum diameters are 105.48 / 106.10 pixels. Normalizing by the existing 0.38 drum diameter gives projected setbacks 0.344 / 0.388. The construction value is 0.36, so the drive axis is at Z=0.77 rather than the drum's Z=1.13. Y=0 is a common-plane construction assumption, not a reconstructed exact source ordinate.

Projected housing/drum diameter ratios are 1.33 / 1.21; outer case diameter 0.50 ±0.05 is the manual estimate. The axial front core depth estimate is 0.208 and rear cone length 0.348; code uses 0.21 and 0.34. Source views at 9–11s expose the fan-like stator, copper windings, retaining straps, twin bearing plates and separate small drum bearing. These are procedural meshes, not hidden end-cap discs or borrowed retail assets.

The source before fixture is the exact original Dynamo function from main, so baseline geometry and attack comparisons include the old drive, not merely a changed X coordinate. Three.js visual object/geometry UUID allocation consumes Math.random; attack parity begins with identical combat random streams after Actor construction, and never changes production RNG. The visible stator test casts 24 rays from the outside through its aperture and requires at least 18 first hits to be copper. Dimensions are checked against real geometry vertices rather than metadata. The same fold part carries the stationary drive/supports, and the unchanged native drum/caps alone use the spin channel.

Drive candidate after adding the guard tab and reducing redundant winding subdivisions: body 4,105 → 6,315 vertices and 8,441 → 12,849 triangles (+4,408); total weapon triangles 15,706 remain below the unchanged 16,000 model guard. The obsolete coaxial case is removed, not layered underneath. It remains merged into the existing body/hinge draw structure. All 26 local source-composition tests pass with zero skips, including all-65 material-boundary LOD. Candidate far body has 674 triangles, no new mixed-material/colour triangles, and stable cached identity. Eight actual source-motion Actors in near/far ABBA order will record geometry bytes, draw calls and raw software-WebGL timing; this is not a full-stage/device performance claim. Final exact-head source/emitted tests, close-up valid-IBL/native images, four-direction real-Actor motion and native post evidence are required before readiness.
