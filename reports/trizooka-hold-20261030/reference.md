## Observed differences and verification targets

1. **Holding relationship.** In 01 and 03 the right hand wraps a downward green rear grip with its index near the red trigger, while the left wraps a separate forward green support at the yellow collar. The barrel is beside the right side of the head, around shoulder/head height. The rear housing continues behind the head. Exact shoulder-to-body contact is obscured. Baseline `_applyPose()` positions the model at `(-.11, .96, .25)` and `fit()` translates it until both arms can reach self-defined sockets; `sockets()` uses the same hand rotation for both grips. Reach-sphere and socket-error success establishes consistency with those chosen targets, not their correctness relative to original anatomy or the visible grip axes. Compare front, rear, side and oblique views with level and pitched aim; inspect grip hole and fingers as well as shoulder/face clearance and long silhouette.
2. **Major shapes.** 00 and 01 show an elongated gray ribbed central tube, prominent faceted yellow collars, an enlarged rounded green muzzle housing, red rim and dark recessed bore. 03 reveals a substantial yellow rear housing with longitudinal silver ribs. Baseline `createTrizooka()` uses a `.42`-long thin central tube, simple `.19`-radius short front pipe and a solid ink-colored disc at the bore; it has no comparable large rear ribbed housing. This is a source-to-reference structural comparison; exact scaling and perspective cannot be determined from the stills. The existing 2026-10-10 diagnostic `reports/inkwave-special-presentation-2026-10-10/render-review.jpg` visually shows a short launcher with a flat colored muzzle, but is a historical capture rather than proof of the current build.
3. **Rear capsules and lines.** 00 shows three substantial yellow capsules with dark bands and elbowed black hoses entering the launcher. In the baseline `createTrizooka()` the three smaller yellow tubes and their hoses are merged into the static housing. 02 shows a capsule ejected, leaving fewer in place, while baseline separate ejected-cartridge visual does not hide the merged static tubes. Verify whether any correction preserves actual shot count and event lifecycle; the still cannot establish precise ejection timing or color behavior for every team.

The current `getMuzzle` and `getAimMuzzle` read the visible model's muzzle. Changing model placement or length can therefore change shot origin unless the existing gameplay launch contract is preserved independently. This risk is from source inspection, not an original-hardware measurement. Compare gameplay origin and projectile trajectory separately from visual barrel alignment.
