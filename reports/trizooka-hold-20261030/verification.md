# Independent Trizooka hold verification

The existing model placed a compact launcher across the rider's face and rendered a flat orange muzzle disk. Its hand-hole error was close to zero, showing that socket residual alone did not establish the correct pose or model. Official reference stills show a longer ribbed launcher with a recessed dark bore, three rear tanks, a rear right-hand trigger grip, and a left support grip farther forward. Reference provenance and the limits of the comparison are documented in the companion reference report.

The controlled comparison uses the source-loaded Character, actual `Actor.update`, native Physics and Projectiles, and FX on a flat floor. It uses Chromium 154.0.8037.57 software WebGL at 480×480, seed `20261030`, and the same frozen browser driver for both builds. It samples neutral, pitch ±0.55 radians, yaw 0.65 radians, movement and a real first-volley recoil; after expiry it checks that the normal weapon and source upper-body owner return. Front, true-front, opposite-front, side, back and device-only views prevent a single camera from being mistaken for face clearance or a hidden grip. The full driver records actual bone, eye, grip and nozzle transforms, native muzzle, real projectile births, paint stamps and an isolated near-wall LOS check. The evidence is local-engine behavior, not Switch performance or exact retail parity.

Baseline `67f708b6` content hash: `cab72739888ee5da1ded95c845b1e0b481d62e0439b7aa4d2a975889b2cbd49c`; matching driver SHA-256: `6788531d7061839fd85ebbd2c1b3fd5be4273fd86f0f98a83c16fdf486f7de62`. The initial candidate attempted to retain the old authoritative launch anchor while moving the visible model. It placed the visible nozzle 0.3374 game units from the native muzzle at neutral (0.1880 forward and 0.2802 lateral), with a range of 0.2390–0.4257 across the sampled states. The actual first volley was born 0.3531 units from the visible muzzle. A real Physics wall placed between the endpoints blocked actor-to-visible-nozzle LOS while actor-to-legacy-muzzle LOS stayed clear. This candidate was rejected for muzzle continuity; the provisional output remains in `/workspace/scratch/trizooka-review-validated-initial/`.

The final emitted build has content hash `d76c80e7ba56743fb6ea3e01bac87b95072b7760351cb3fd243f0e66b5779690`, source `special-motion.mjs` SHA-256 `a4336340325fb796b0dfa6f21382915a37ef22531078f2857ebebc5b26b13ff2`, and artifact revision `d34673c42881432f93416f2f30f68c3c7df17e36d5e918f7dba25fedd88f0c0e`. The comparison completed without browser or FX director errors, with the source-motion bank ready and normal weapon restored at expiry.

The final neutral trigger hand is at y=0.810 and the support hand at y=0.960, with the support 0.26 game units ahead along the barrel. Each authored handle axis is surrounded by actual green mesh vertices (18 rear, 36 forward), and at every sampled pitch, yaw, movement and recoil point the appropriate source-skeleton hand hole lies on its axis within `5e-16` units. The barrel direction aligns with the sampled aim vector; at neutral the barrel axis is 0.443 units to the actor's right of the eye midpoint and 0.007 above it. These are controlled rig measurements, not retail dimensions. Both-eye clearance in the true-front and opposite-front screenshots is visible, while the weapon-side angle can occlude the face because of perspective.

The final native muzzle and visible nozzle coincide in each unobstructed sample. All three actual first-volley projectiles were born exactly at the visible nozzle in this fixture. [First-shot effects close-up](visual/final-first-shot-effects.png) is heavily overexposed by the flash, so it cannot establish trail continuity by itself; the synchronous birth/nozzle measurements supply the position evidence. [Recoil side view](visual/final-recoil-side.png) shows the held model with the FX layer hidden. With a thin real Physics wall at z=0.5472 blocking actor-to-visible-nozzle LOS, native `_muzzle` fell back exactly to actor-relative `[0, 1.05, 0.3]`. This is an isolated LOS/fallback probe. The additional getter, volley, cadence and scalar contracts are covered in [anchor-audit.md](anchor-audit.md); that audit does not certify all combat or collision outcomes in arbitrary scenes.

Moving the real muzzle intentionally changes gameplay coordinates. In the controlled moving shot, each first-volley birth shifted 0.40813 game units and its velocity vector changed by 0.69787 units/s relative to the baseline. Both runs produced 50 paint stamps with identical recorded radius/team scalars, but corresponding stamp coordinates differ by up to 0.52181 units. The sampled actor position, velocity, gauge and ammo matched, and all three recorded projectile damage, radius and lifetime values matched. These observations do not imply identical damage or paint outcomes in every scene. The complete sampled data is in [before.json](visual/before.json) and [final.json](visual/final.json); full angle, aim and recoil images remain in `/workspace/scratch/trizooka-review-final/`.
