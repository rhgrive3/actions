# Verification status

- Original VTF decoding: all RGBA channels of both 16-frame DXT3 textures matched an independent Pillow DDS decode, 0 differing bytes. Original Git checkout remained unchanged.
- Original baked pack: 32 frames × 2 LODs passed source-pixel comparison, finite/nonzero triangles, consistently wound watertight edges and positive volume checks. Unsafe simplification is rejected rather than weakening the geometry assertions.
- Orthographic alpha comparison: both patterns and all 16 frames were rendered and compared at 512×512 in Chromium and WebKit. Chromium's maximum mean absolute alpha error is 0.187809 **byte units**, or 0.0737% of full alpha; maximum changed support is 1315/262144 pixels (0.502%). WebKit's respective maxima are 0.153545 bytes and 1449/262144 pixels (0.553%). Largest Chromium individual error is 255 on a small boundary feature. These raster differences are documented, not called pixel-identical rendering. The stored source RGBA remains exact.

Actual native Roller input in the emitted Practice Range produced the new volume, continued native flight, landed and cleared its heads.
