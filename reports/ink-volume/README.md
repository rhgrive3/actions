# INKWAVE alpha volume engine — enabled in the production game

Work began from latest main `bf38901475fa857016cf06bcc98cd764898795d0` on 2026-10-11. PR #401 was not inspected or changed. The upstream `inkwave-public/` source remains intact; the existing production source composition adds the renderer.

## Architecture and integration

`patches/local-quality/ink-volume/` separates VTF decoding, alpha/distance analysis and mesh construction, pack reading, GPU batches and weapon recipes. `ink-volume-adapter.mjs` connects FX-owned registration/disposal/lighting/telemetry to the actual Projectiles `_draw()` pass. The firing, flight, collision, damage, paint and network packet logic retain their existing owners and values. The 2026-10-12 extension adopts latest main `90b4fea706d6018fcf48dc205198f19194fd5886` and connects horizontal/vertical Rollers, Brushes, Shooter/Dualies/Splatling rods, and Slosher/Tri-Slosher sheets. Other projectile owners keep their current rendering unless their own compatible pack is explicitly registered.

`recipes.mjs` classifies immutable weapon/family/mode at local and JSON-wire births. Catalogue packets are observed before the outer runtime restores its descriptor, so their received mode is used too; the current owner's weapon or swing never decides an already-fired round. Rod timing/width and sheet width/tail use existing S3 presentation helpers and explicit catalogue draw records. Source sparse fields retain the native fallback; they are not reconstructed official defaults. Roller vertical uses its sourced S3 draw radius with the unchanged Roller artwork: SplatoonSWEPs has no first-generation vertical-flick asset. This adaptation does not certify a Nintendo vertical-flick silhouette.

Shooter packs contain `crosshair/line.vtf` and `crosshair/dot.vtf`, in that order. The original `RenderGeneral` beam uses U=0.3..0.7 and its tip is 0.75 of beam width. The offline tool clips a closed 3D beam to that same source UV window, while keeping **every original RGBA byte** in the pack. The two ends and beam form an atomic composite. They follow the round's world-space tail direction, never the camera. Slosher uses one continuous stretched sheet with its launch-plane orientation, without appended beads. Nonuniform instance transforms use inverse-transpose normals. The shared two sheet patterns remain 16 frames each; no new texture is drawn or randomized.

`submitComposite(source, parts)` accepts 1–8 reused parts with `name`, positive `size`, `pattern`, optional world `position`, positive `stretch` vector and unit `rotation` quaternion. Reservation checks the entire visible effect against both instance and triangle budgets before writing any batch. A failed reservation emits no partial volume, allowing the existing renderer to draw the complete effect. Preflight matrices and recipe vectors are reused, and unregister/dispose clears cached batch references.

Both local and replicated projectile births snapshot the shot's weapon identity and world direction. Replicated births are observed after the complete native metadata restore: current vertical and horizontal heads share nose/tail settings, so those visual settings cannot classify modern packets. The immutable received mode is used even if the owner's current weapon/mode changed. The existing projectile generation guards pooling; a WeakMap holds each rendering orientation without keeping dead rounds alive. A source is submitted at its actual `p.pos` and `p.age`; camera changes and gravity do not rotate the sheet toward the viewer. Visibility culling, triangle and instance caps only limit presentation. Over-budget heads keep the existing game renderer rather than affecting scoring or collision.

The engine exposes `FX.registerInkVolume(name, decodedPack)` and `FX.loadInkVolume(name, manifestURL)`. Additional weapons can add a small recipe calling `engine.submit(name, source, worldSize, pattern)` or `submitComposite` in their existing render pass. Pack dimensions, frame count and FPS are independent of weapon physics. Gameplay does not run VTF decoding, contour analysis, simplification or mesh construction.

## Geometry and original pixels

The decoder supports VTF 7.0–7.2 RGBA8888/BGRA8888 and DXT1/3/5 with explicit rejection of unsupported environment-map/depth layouts. It reads every original high-resolution frame, after the thumbnail and lower mip levels, without changing source bytes. Both Roller textures are DXT3. An independent Pillow DDS decode compared **all RGBA channels of all 32 frames: zero differences**.

A padded source-resolution alpha lattice clips triangles at half an alpha byte. Empty areas, internal holes and isolated low-alpha components remain separate. Covered interior tiles share edge cuts; adaptive triangulation has no T junctions. The front and reversed back share UVs/XY; every open front edge generates two side triangles, including hole edges. Chamfer distance from the original support mask controls local width and thickness. Only this derived field is smoothed to round medial-axis ridges; the original image/alpha is never blurred, redrawn, normalized or replaced. An upper-position bias and asymmetric back ratio distinguish broad lobes and thin membranes. All thickness/shape settings are INKWAVE art parameters, not official values.

The offline simplifier locks every source contour and its first interior ring. It works on the front 2D triangulation with height as an attribute; the back reuses that topology. Folded/zero-area candidates protect the implicated original neighborhood and retry; unsafe candidates retain the original geometry. It never prunes disconnected components. Both LODs retain all original boundaries, holes, source pixels and 16 animation frames. Normal vectors are evaluated from the same height field before simplification and stored as normalized signed 16-bit attributes. The runtime also accepts full-precision normals. The material uses world orientation, real geometry normals, environment light and restrained specular; source alpha remains separate from light/gloss.

The alpha/RGB filter fringe stays thin and transitions smoothly into the thicker body. Stretching that fringe over the bevel previously created a black strip at grazing angles. Front/back UVs stay unchanged; dedicated side UV seams sample the nearest covered original texel, preserving its alpha. Side normals match the rounded surface rim. Exact-position welding verifies a closed physical shell despite UV seams. Image-edge clipping and per-frame half-texel atlas bounds prevent sampling a neighboring animation frame.

Registration assembles source pixels into separate per-pattern atlases with no mipmap resampling. It creates all 32 shared frame geometries × 2 LODs before shooting. Each pattern shares one texture and two materials: opaque source pixels write depth, while original partial alpha blends against that depth. These are two passes of the same 3D surface. Conservative offline triangle partitions restrict each pass; geometry and instance attributes share GPU buffers and a combined index buffer. The default cap is 128 instances and 600k submitted volume triangles, scaled by the existing FX quality multiplier. Distance LOD is 22 game units; culling is 65 units. These are tunable rendering settings. The engine has no new WebGL extension requirement beyond the game's current Three/WebGL renderer.

## Rights and original-source findings

Local checkout: `https://github.com/zerokizo/splatoonsweps`, commit `a74592726f38f26fd1d3a15ed315701068283847`. Its README describes Wii U/first-generation Splatoon and requires Team Fortress 2 and Enhanced Inklings. No repository LICENSE was found during source inspection. The user subsequently confirmed that asset permission had been obtained and instructed ordinary default activation. The four required VTFs and their VMTs are now included unchanged in `reports/ink-volume/source/`; their source-derived packs ship with the game. Other inspected models/PCF/ring assets are not needed for this renderer and are not bundled.

Inspected files:

- `materials/splatoonsweps/effects/rollerink1.vtf`: 256×256, 16 DXT3 frames, one mip. `rollerink2.vtf`: 128×128, otherwise same animation layout. Both corresponding VMTs use UnlitGeneric, translucent, no culling, vertex color/alpha.
- `lua/effects/SplatoonSWEPsShooterInk.lua`: selects one of the two patterns; `RenderSplash` uses `min(floor(lifetime*30),15)`, holds the last frame and draws a quad at five times DrawRadius. Init captures `-LocalPlayer view forward` plus perturbation **once**, not every render. INKWAVE uses shot-world direction instead of copying that spectator-dependent initialization.
- `SplatoonSWEPsRollerSplash.lua`: PCF muzzle effect follows the `roll` attachment (`tip` for brushes, `spout` for the alternate flag). It is distinct from the flying Roller VTF sheet.
- `SplatoonSWEPsMuzzleRing.lua`, `inkring.vtf`, `inkringn.vtf`, and both ring VMTs: ring is a separate procedural curved strip with lifetime/angle/width changes, not the Roller sprite. Ring textures are 128×256, one DXT5 frame, nine mip levels. No muzzle-ring or original projectile physics was transplanted.
- `particles/splatoonsweps.pcf`: parsed as binary DMX 2 / PCF 1 using srctools. Roller splash is a particle-system definition with maximum 1000 and `particle/particle_glow_01.vmt`; rolling references `particle/antlion_goop3/antlion_goop3.vmt`, plus `particle_glow_05_additive`. These are external Source-game particle materials, not this engine's mesh specification.
- Splat Roller Source MDL v48 view/world models: viewmodel has 43 bones with `root_1 → neck_1 → roll_root_1 → roll_1`; `roll` attachment is on bone 41 (`roll_root_1`). Left/right world models have 5 bones, with `roll` on bone 3. Model coordinates/attachments were inspected locally, but none replaces INKWAVE's current rig or muzzle.

The supplied `splatoonsweps_RollerSplash_MuzzleRing_v6_ATTACHMENT_FIX.html` (SHA-256 `6300b626d3efd3e3a28f07071507a2ce7ee8e9be487cccb4065bd9f699ac9e7f`) was fully separated into its five scripts and inspected locally. Its embedded two 4×4 PNG sheets match all 32 original alpha frames and all visible RGB bytes; RGB in fully transparent pixels differs, so this engine decodes the VTF directly. The prototype draws six vertices per flying quad without depth testing or geometry normals; its enhanced mode bakes RGB highlights into a Canvas image. Neither technique is used for the volume. Its default OSS mode captures a camera-derived normal once, while its optional velocity mode changes orientation during flight. This engine snapshots world shot direction once.

The prototype's 12-shot first-generation physics, `UNIT=.045`, approximate birth-to-muzzle interpolation and demonstration hand root `[0,.62,-1.11]` are not INKWAVE gameplay parameters. Its model-rest `roll` attachment metadata and separate 672-triangle MuzzleRing/16 PCF particles were cross-checked; the original/ enhanced front, side, oblique and top views were rendered locally. The optional external Valve glow dependency was unavailable in this comparison and remained hidden, with no substitute. The HTML and its embedded images are local-only. Splatoon 3 Ver.11.3.0 exact visual/physical-device footage is not available here; this work does not claim a retail-perfect match or official thickness, shine, FPS, physics or geometry values.

## Ordinary startup and reproduction

Build and start the game normally. FX automatically loads the included Roller and Shooter packs; both complete in the existing warmup phase before shader compilation and the first shot. No URL setting, console command or opt-in is required. Original source/geometry bytes use lossless gzip (17,636,846 + 57,916 bytes, **16.875 MiB** combined) with SHA-256 over the uncompressed pack. The 34 source RGBA frames and all mesh bytes remain unchanged. VTF decoding and mesh construction are offline-only modules, excluded from the emitted runtime graph; their source hashes remain bound to the build identity.

```sh
npm install --no-save --no-package-lock esbuild@0.28.2 playwright@1.62.1 meshoptimizer@1.3.0
node scripts/build-inkwave.mjs inkwave-public .built-site/volume
node scripts/check-inkwave-projectile-volumes.mjs .built-site/volume \
  patches/local-quality/ink-volume/assets/roller \
  patches/local-quality/ink-volume/assets/shooter \
  .browser-evidence/projectiles --default --no-performance
```

FX exposes `inkVolumeReady` (status only) and `inkVolumeError` for diagnostics. Disposal aborts pending network work, releases readiness and rejects late registration. A network/decode failure preserves existing gameplay and rendering; it does not require an enabling setting. URLs resolve beside the versioned pack module, including subdirectory hosting. Manifest and binary share an origin; decoded size and SHA-256 are checked before registration. Invalid cached data is evicted and fetched again, and storage/quota failures do not discard a verified usable pack.

The existing 10 MiB startup snapshot and 12 MiB revision budget stay unchanged. Both small pack manifests enter its precache. Compressed binaries use a separate content-addressed CacheStorage with two slots (one per family, at most 20 MiB each); a successful first game load warms them automatically and evicts a prior version in the same slot. Already loaded assets work in later offline launches without a new per-code-revision binary copy. A fresh offline install that has not previously downloaded these packs can still use the existing renderer. The current combined transport/cache payload is 16.875 MiB, not part of the 10 MiB snapshot, and its first online download adds startup/network cost.

Asset registration remains available for extensions, not for enabling the bundled feature. `FX.registerInkVolume(name, decodedPack)` / `FX.loadInkVolume(name, manifestURL)` accept compatible new packs. Individual mappings use `projectile:<weapon-id>:<recipe>` (`roller-horizontal`, `roller-vertical`, `brush`, `shooter`, `slosher`). Machine vortices, bubbles, explosions and Dread Wringer require their own art rather than an unrelated sheet.

To reproduce the bake, use the unchanged source VTFs in `reports/ink-volume/source/` with `bake-inkwave-volumes.mjs`. `bake-inkwave-projectile-volumes.mjs` takes a SplatoonSWEPs checkout. `package-inkwave-volume-packs.mjs <sheet-bake> <rod-bake>` performs the reviewed lossless transport conversion. `verify-inkwave-volume-pack.mjs` accepts either uncompressed research bakes or shipped gzip packs. Source hashes, 34 RGBA comparisons, gzip limits/corruption, default automatic boot, cache reuse/repair/eviction and late disposal are covered by tests.

CI retains authored structural fixtures for generic topology tests, verifies every original shipped mesh and pixel against the committed source VTF, and renders the native nine-action game with its **default original assets**. Review images are in this report and CI artifacts; they are source-based implementation evidence, not a claim of Nintendo retail-perfect imagery.

## Verification and limits

Results and measurements are recorded in `results.md` / `measurements.json`. Front comparison uses orthographic rendered alpha at 512×512 for all 32 original frames. Byte-for-byte source preservation, mesh validity and rendered raster agreement are separate checks; rasterization at clipped/bilinear edge cells need not be byte-identical to a full plane. Each frame additionally receives side/top/bottom alpha and RGB readback against its truly opaque source regions: every eroded body pixel must remain opaque and avoid a black seam. Six camera directions and an oblique 16-frame image sequence are saved locally. The native game run fires through real input in the Practice Range, follows flight/landing, compares its existing renderer against the registered volume renderer, and checks repeated match resets.

No arbitrary new physics, damage, paint or muzzle constants enter gameplay. Physical iPhone/Android/iPad measurements, retail Ver.11.3.0 equivalence, unrestricted overlap order for translucent instancing remain explicit limitations. Opaque bodies have correct depth occlusion; partial-alpha fringes do not implement order-independent transparency. The original pack uses substantial memory and cannot be described as mobile-validated. The shipped packs are automatically downloaded and cached separately as described above; initial offline use and physical-device memory/thermal behavior still need separate validation.

CI/base alignment: during review, main advanced to `b078c379` with the separate owned-run paint scan change. Its workflow referenced a new test while checking out the older feature SHA. That six-file main update is adopted intact; no scan test is removed or replaced. The combined build passes the strict paint and volume packaging gates. Renderer core is unchanged, and measurements retain their original implementation/base scope.
