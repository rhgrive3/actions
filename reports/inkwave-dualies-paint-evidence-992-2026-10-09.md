## Changed behavior

`dualiesImpactPaintSource()` in `runtime/weapons-fidelity.mjs` now resolves lateral floor radius from the retained near/middle/far source fields. The implementation uses the existing projectile-start-to-impact distance convention and the explicit `weaponsFidelityCompletion.worldUnitsPerSourceUnit`, currently 1. This remains INKWAVE's world calibration, not an SI or pixel-perfect Switch measurement.

Straight-flight floor impacts also resolve the source angle depth envelope. The existing rasterizer receives radius and `stretchAmt=depthScale-1` through its real first gameplay splat. The direction is projected onto the contacted plane before normalization so the already-accounted incidence angle is not attenuated twice. Exactly normal incidence preserves the rasterizer's lack of a preferred in-plane direction. Existing seed, native random draw order, paint authority, returned CPU area and score credit are retained. The temporary interception restores the original method even when paint throws.

Previously every terminal Dualies shot used its generic radius multiplied by random 0.85–1.15 and fixed 0.7 stretch. At source distances 1.1 / 10.55 / 20, lateral radii are now 1.71 / 1.685 / 1.66 in the current world scale. At straight-phase incidence 10 / 22.5 / 35 degrees, depth coefficients are 2.24 / 1.775 / 1.31. Contact-normal floor admission follows the existing floor/wall convention. Wall contacts still enter their separate sourced wall-drop state.

## Remaining acceptance, not marked complete

#992 remains open. This patch does **not** identify the original break/free reference-height quantity, its composition with angle, or the seven-pattern normal-shot droplet schedule. Existing break/free depth and generic flight droplets remain. The source endpoints, one-droplet maximum and SplitNum are known; treating every shot as the nearest droplet or inventing the seven offsets would not recover the missing algorithm. The old issue comment promised a droplet-only implementation, but no such live Dualies path or associated commit was present at this baseline.

No Switch gameplay capture, actual browser GPU comparison, console paint texture, or source-coordinate absolute scale measurement was performed. Passing the following tests proves the INKWAVE source/CPU integration and reproducibility, not full S3 fidelity.
