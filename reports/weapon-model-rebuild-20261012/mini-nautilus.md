## References and the errors they exposed

The original working files from PR1246 commit2555b20 were a 400x400 Nautilus47 still and a 1280x960 rotation sheet containing twelve 320x320 cells, six distinct views repeated twice. `alt-kits.json` establishes that the turntable is **Nautilus79**, not47. The analogous Mini turntable is an alternate finish too. They can guide visible shared form, not prove base-kit materials.

The Nautilus coil, return loop and angular tank were already visible in the79 sheet. They were incorrectly interpreted as a small concentric hub and omitted. This was a modeling error, not just a missing reference. The first corrected reverse adds these components. The second makes the tank larger/angular/thicker and attempts flat strip sections. Review then found every custom coil normal was inward, while the separate return-frame hole contour also had inconsistent winding. The second candidate was therefore not accepted. A position/UV-identical winding-only control isolated the culling error, before any subsequent section or connection changes.

Later verified references:

- [Nintendo Splatoon Base, Nautilus47 image](https://www.nintendo.com/jp/character/splatoon/images/en/sp/fashion/weapon/detail/spinner/weapon_image02.png), 1252x608. It shows the outer central disc as reflective silver, not proven black paint.
- [Official Squid Research Lab S2 announcement](https://splatoonus.tumblr.com/post/175812736278/version-320-is-scheduled-to-be-released-this), July12,2018. Its1200x675 promo includes a clear47 reverse with a hollow coil and pale angular tank.
- Original S3 Nautilus79 turntable sheet at `reports/inkwave-weapon-model-workspace/rotsheets/s3-spinner-serein.rot.jpg` in PR1246. This supplies a second reverse direction and demonstrates the earlier omission independently of the newS2 source.

Exact **Splatoon3 Nautilus47 reverse geometry/materials remain unverified**. S2 and79 evidence does not establish complete S3 47 identity. Retail cameras, focal lengths and manufacturing dimensions are unknown. Comparisons use corresponding face/axis/neck landmarks, fixed candidate cameras and a common before/after crop; they do not claim pixel parity.

Approximate same-image vertical extents give tank-including-depth / coil ratios of about.83 in theS2 oblique and1.07 in the low-resolution79 side view, each with several-pixel boundary uncertainty and perspective/depth bias. These constrain the design, not exact dimensions. The initial candidate's nominal tank height/coil diameter was.077/.122=.63; the next is.103/.1166=.88 before tank rotation and projected extent. The nominal tank depth is.044m. Its center moves forward and down while weapon length, muzzle and hands remain fixed.

## Lighting correction before material judgment

The old gallery supplied a32x16 equirectangular texture. Bundled PMREM derives cubeSize8/maxMip3, below the CubeUV shader's minimum mip4/tile16. Both native and standard metal shaders darkened under that invalid input. Valid1024x512 input restores the intended radiance distribution at cube256. This invalidates the earlier black-chrome rejection and compensatory satin adoption.

Diagnostic30bcb9ad fixes every non-color/non-aMat attribute and index while comparing satin against the4d silver/M.metal distribution. It completed successfully under valid studio IBL and native Environment day, Neutral tone mapping, exposure.94 and the real high post stack. Broad hardware returns to metal. A separate cap-only comparison changes the central cap from black/satin to silver/metal, retaining its geometry. Dark grips, bearing gap, rib recesses and nozzle throats stay dark. Team-ink channels and the charge tell remain gameplay conventions, not claims of exact retail materials.

Separate inclusive instrumentation does not establish a single cause; GC, JIT, scheduler/runner and model-related effects are not resolved. No repeated runs are used to search for favorable numbers.

New geometry needs its own exact-head final measurement and visual review. No speedup, full-match FPS, unchanged performance or final fidelity is claimed. No merge or deployment is performed by this task.
