Winner input, numeric labels, timing, introductory racing animation, authoritative coverage, score and online receipt stay with their existing owners.
- #715: the actual HUD reads its existing local Actor form each update. `form === 'squid'` hides only the aiming reticle. Weapon/charge/spread/target state keeps updating behind that visibility gate, so returning to humanoid restores the correct current reticle even after a weapon change. Hit/splat indicators, ink tank, shield and sub-aim UI are not hidden as a group. No Actor/weapon/input state is changed.
